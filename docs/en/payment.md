# How to Pay for the Hosting

Payments go through your client area at **[my.amnezia.host](https://my.amnezia.host)** — the “Invoices” section. Three payment services are available; pick whichever method suits you.

| Service | Payment methods |
| :--- | :--- |
| **FreeKassa** | Russian bank cards, SBP |
| **YooKassa** | YooMoney account, cash payment |
| **Stripe** | international bank cards |

## Payment steps {#how}

1.  Sign in to your client area at **[my.amnezia.host](https://my.amnezia.host)**.
2.  Open “Invoices” and pick the unpaid invoice — or click **Renew Service** on the server card.
3.  Choose a payment service and complete the payment.
4.  The service renews automatically once the payment is confirmed.

## Invoices and history {#invoices}

Every issued invoice and the full payment history are kept in the “Invoices” section of the client area, where payment documents can also be downloaded.

## Prices and periods {#prices}

Current plans, available billing periods, and prices are on **[amnezia.host](https://amnezia.host)**. The billing period can be changed through support: **[How to change the billing period](/en/billing-period)**.

::: tip The payment failed
Try another payment service from the table — a decline usually comes from the bank rather than the hosting. If none of them work, open a ticket: **[Contacting Support](/en/support)**.
:::
