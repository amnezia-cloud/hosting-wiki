# How to Get a Refund for a Server

Refunds are handled through a ticket in your client area at **[my.amnezia.host](https://my.amnezia.host)** — support will guide you through the process.

## Refund terms {#terms}

| Term the server was ordered for | When a refund is possible |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1 month** | On the day of purchase or the next day |
| **3, 6, or 12 months** | If **at least one month** of the paid period remains |

Full terms are in the **[Refund and Compensation Policy](https://amnezia.host/en/refund-policy)**.

## How to request it {#how}

1.  Open a ticket in your client area at **[my.amnezia.host](https://my.amnezia.host)**.
2.  State the service (IP address or server number), the purchase date, and the reason for the refund.
3.  Wait for support to confirm the calculated amount and process the refund.

::: tip First check whether the problem is fixable
If the reason is that the VPN will not connect, the cause is often ISP-level blocking rather than the server — and it is usually solved by changing the protocol or port. Before requesting a refund, look at the **[step-by-step troubleshooting](/en/vpn-troubleshooting)** or write to support: **[details checklist](/en/support#checklist)**.
:::
