# How to Change the Billing Period

The billing period is changed by support — it cannot be switched on your own in the client area.

## Where to ask {#where}

*   **The support Telegram bot** — the quick option.
*   **A ticket in your client area** at **[my.amnezia.host](https://my.amnezia.host)** — when you want a written record for the service.

In your message state the service (IP address or server number) and the period you want to move to.

## Available periods and prices {#prices}

The list of billing periods and the price of each are on **[amnezia.host](https://amnezia.host)**. Longer periods are usually cheaper per month.

## Things to keep in mind {#notes}

*   A period change applies to the next invoice — the currently paid term is not recalculated.
*   Refunds on early cancellation depend on the chosen term: **[refund conditions](/en/refund#terms)**.
*   You can renew the service on the current period yourself with the **Renew Service** button on the server card: **[How to pay for the hosting](/en/payment)**.
